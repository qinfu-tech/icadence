## 1.13 — September 17, 2026 / 2026 年 9 月 17 日

### English

- Added Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for upcoming breaks, actions, and Quiet Period status.
- Added Quiet Periods with quick durations, custom schedules, and repeat options.
- Improved walking verification guidance and progress tracking.
- Improved reminders across midnight and long countdown displays.
- Fixed occasional blank widgets and improved stability.

### 简体中文

- 新增主屏幕与锁定屏幕小组件，查看下一次休息、当前动作和免提醒状态。
- 新增免提醒时段，支持快捷时长、自定义时间和重复设置。
- 优化走动验证提示与进度显示，运动时间从 0 秒开始平滑累计。
- 优化跨日提醒和长时间倒计时显示。
- 修复小组件偶发空白及其他稳定性问题。
